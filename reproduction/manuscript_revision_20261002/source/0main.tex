\begin{abstract}
\par\noindent\rev{\textbf{Background.} Chinese job advertisements express competencies through coordinated phrases and context-dependent terms. Comparing extraction systems requires consistent decisions about which characters form each mention and which competency type it expresses.}
\par\noindent\rev{\textbf{Methods.} We developed the Chinese-SkillSpan benchmark from a corpus of 22,840 sentences across four recruitment collections. Character-level annotations distinguish language, knowledge, occupational skills, and transversal competences. An ESCO-informed handbook was refined through human coding, disagreement analysis, and adjudication to specify annotation scope, boundaries, and types. The resource separates a frozen human reference from LLM-assisted Silver supervision. Silver checks combined automated structural validation with human review of samples before and after bulk annotation. Three annotators independently applied the final handbook to a separate random sample, blinded to machine suggestions and one another's labels. We compared prompted models, supervised adaptation, and multilingual encoders.}
\par\noindent\rev{\textbf{Results.} Silver-supervised Qwen adaptation improved typed exact micro-F1 on the human reference from a no-adapter baseline of 0.361 to $0.540\pm0.035$ (mean $\pm$ sample SD across three training seeds) under fixed inference rules. Each adapted model also improved exact recall across all assessed span-length groups. Independent blinded coding showed higher agreement on boundaries alone than on typed spans, indicating additional difficulty in type assignment.}
\par\noindent\rev{\textbf{Conclusions.} Chinese-SkillSpan pairs source-text spans with explicit annotation rules and separate human and Silver labels, enabling model comparisons and inspection of boundary and type errors. Human-reference results concern data used in guideline development; generalization to unseen advertisements requires independent testing.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Data Availability}
\begin{sloppypar}
\RaggedRight % [R4-LAYOUT] Avoid stretched word spacing around intact URLs.
% [R39-DAS] Two-paragraph access statement; detailed version/model inventory remains in Appendix D.
The \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22698504}{dataset archive (v0.1.3; DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22698504)} contains the corpus, handbook, 150-sentence human reference, and Silver-plus B2 training/development files; \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22288337}{10.5281/zenodo.22288337} is the concept DOI. The \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/blob/main/reproduction/evidence_review_20260925/annotation_layers/README.md}{historical annotation-layer release} separately provides pseudonymized coding and QA150 exports with original text and offsets. The \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/tree/main/reproduction/agreement/finalguide_abc_20261002}{independent blinded three-annotator study} provides frozen labels, source identifiers, agreement code, and results. These later annotation studies are outside the original Zenodo archive.

The \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22851581}{Qwen reproduction archive (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22851581)} contains three B2 LoRA adapters, selected expanded-Silver checkpoints, predictions, configurations, and scoring tools. Complete texts and labels for the final 9,540- and 9,646-record pools remain unavailable for public retraining. The six Chinese-supervised XLM-R/ESCOXLM-R checkpoints are linked in the \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/tree/0c73207c0f9b00fa87f385b78bcb7342bd00bfcb/results_snapshots/table11_evidence_20260924/HF_RELEASE.md}{Hugging Face model index}; their code, configurations, predictions, and verification records are in the \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22937245}{audit archive (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22937245)}. The \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/blob/main/reproduction/evidence_review_20260925/README.md}{evidence index} separates local rescoring of archived predictions from server-reported checkpoint inference.

The \href{https://huggingface.co/AlfredJames/jobbert-zh}{JobBERT-zh initialization} supplies the domain-adapted encoder and inherited CRF; the \href{https://huggingface.co/AlfredJames/jobbert-zh-v6a}{B2 continuation} supplies continued checkpoints, with seed 42 as its default. The \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark}{project repository} and \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/blob/main/REPRODUCIBILITY.md}{reproduction guide} link code, preprocessing, scoring, and experiment records. Table~\ref{tab:component-access} summarizes access and verification; \hyperref[app:e]{Appendix E} links external-benchmark protocols and results.

Reuse terms vary by component. Explicitly covered software and documentation retain Apache-2.0. Author-controlled annotations and research materials permit academic research and peer review with attribution; this permission excludes third-party recruitment text. Model checkpoints and adapters follow their accompanying notices and base-model licences. The \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/blob/main/DATA_AVAILABILITY.md}{data-access guide} specifies these terms; \hyperref[app:d]{Appendix D} records earlier versions and access limits.
\end{sloppypar}
