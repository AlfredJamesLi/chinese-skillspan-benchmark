\begin{abstract}
\rev{Competency extraction from Chinese job advertisements requires context-sensitive decisions about span boundaries and types, especially in coordinated expressions. We introduce Chinese-SkillSpan, a benchmark combining character-level annotations, an annotation handbook, and a shared evaluation protocol. The handbook draws on the European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations (ESCO) classification to distinguish language, knowledge-related requirements, occupational skills, and transversal competencies. Human coding and adjudication informed the annotation rules. The resource provides human reference annotations for evaluation and machine-generated Silver labels for supervision, with structural checks, sampled human review, and a separate blinded annotation study. We compare prompted language models, Silver-supervised adaptation, and multilingual encoders. On the human reference, Qwen adaptation increased typed exact-span micro-F1 from 0.361 to a mean of 0.540 across three training seeds under the same inference settings. The adapted mean remained below the observed JobBERT-zh + CRF and prompted GPT scores. Blinded annotation achieved mean pairwise typed exact-span F1 of 0.818, with closer agreement on boundaries alone. Because the human reference informed handbook development and shares source documents with training, the model results support diagnostic comparison. The benchmark provides a common basis for comparing extraction systems and examining boundary and type errors.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Data Availability}
\begin{sloppypar}
\RaggedRight % [R4-LAYOUT] Avoid stretched word spacing around intact URLs.

The \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22698504}{dataset archive (v0.1.3; DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22698504)} contains the corpus, annotation handbook, 150-sentence human reference set, and Silver training and development files used by Qwen and the encoder comparison (2,150/169 records). The repository archive series has concept DOI \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22288337}{10.5281/zenodo.22288337}; the version-specific link above identifies the dataset used here. A separate \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/blob/main/reproduction/evidence_review_20260925/annotation_layers/README.md}{historical annotation-layer release} provides human-annotation and additional-review exports with pseudonymized annotator identifiers, original texts, and offsets. The \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/tree/main/reproduction/agreement/finalguide_abc_20261003}{release for the independent blinded three-annotator study} contains frozen individual annotation layers, source identifiers, agreement code, and results. These later annotation releases are maintained separately on GitHub.

The \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22851581}{Qwen reproduction archive (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22851581)} contains the three Qwen LoRA adapters trained on these Silver sets, selected expanded-silver checkpoints, predictions, configurations, and scoring tools. For the adopted 9,540-record expanded Silver pool and the supplementary 9,646-record alternative, public materials comprise split identifiers and selected model outputs. Complete texts and labels are retained locally and are available to editors and reviewers through the corresponding author for confidential peer review. Six XLM-R/ESCOXLM-R checkpoints trained with Chinese supervision are linked from the \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/blob/0c73207c0f9b00fa87f385b78bcb7342bd00bfcb/results_snapshots/table11_evidence_20260924/HF_RELEASE.md}{Hugging Face model index}; their code, configurations, and predictions are provided in the \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22942441}{audit archive (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22942441)}. The \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/blob/main/reproduction/evidence_review_20260925/README.md}{reproduction documentation} distinguishes local rescoring of archived predictions from checkpoint-inference results reported by the server.

The \href{https://huggingface.co/AlfredJames/jobbert-zh}{JobBERT-zh initialization release} provides the domain-adapted encoder and inherited CRF. The \href{https://huggingface.co/AlfredJames/jobbert-zh-v6a}{Silver-trained JobBERT-zh release} provides the continued-training checkpoints, with seed 42 as the default. The \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark}{project repository} and \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/blob/main/REPRODUCIBILITY.md}{reproduction guide} link the code, preprocessing and scoring procedures, and experiment records. Table~\ref{tab:component-access} summarizes component access and verification. External-task protocols and results are retained in the \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/tree/main/reproduction/major_revision_20261004/external_experiments}{supplementary experiment archive}.

Reuse is governed by component-specific terms. Software and documentation explicitly covered by Apache-2.0 retain that licence. Reuse of third-party recruitment text follows the applicable source terms and permissions. Model checkpoints and adapters remain subject to their accompanying notices and base-model licences.
