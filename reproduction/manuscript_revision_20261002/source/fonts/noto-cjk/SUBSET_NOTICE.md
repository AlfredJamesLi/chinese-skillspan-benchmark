# Bundled font subsets

CNSS Submission Serif and CNSS Submission Sans are renamed subsets of Noto CJK.
They retain the glyphs, layout features and metrics required by this manuscript.
The fonts remain licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; copyright metadata
and the accompanying licence files are retained. New Chinese characters may require
regenerating the subsets or using the complete original fonts.
