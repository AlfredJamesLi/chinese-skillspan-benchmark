# Editable tables

Table_01.tex through Table_24.tex are standalone table documents. Compile them
with XeLaTeX from this archive's root directory. They use table_support.tex,
wlpeerj.cls, the bundled fonts, and tex/stage2_result_values.tex. The shared
support file supplies citation labels matching the manuscript reference list.

The standalone tables use US Letter pages with one-inch margins. Tables 9 and
10 use their own font and spacing settings to fit the annotation rules on single
pages. All captions, rules, examples and numerical values are retained. These
standalone layouts do not control the table layout in the complete manuscript.

## Source key for Tables 9 and 10

E: ESCO skills hierarchy and transversal concepts (European Commission, 2024).
Q: EQF definitions (Council of the European Union, 2017).
Z: Related annotation guidance in SkillSpan, Appendix B (Zhang et al., 2022b).
I: Unitization and categorization in agreement analysis (Artstein and Poesio, 2008).
P: The project's V4.2.14 operational decision.

Combined marks indicate adaptation rather than verbatim rules. Chinese examples
and exact boundaries are project illustrations. The cited works are listed in
the manuscript reference list.
