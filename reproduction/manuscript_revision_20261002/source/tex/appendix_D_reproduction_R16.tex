\subsection{Source Categories and Data Preparation}
The four original collection labels describe acquisition batches; the three acquisition routes identify MacroData, public-institution collection, and Tianchi. Acquisition batches and routes classify the same material at different levels. The original training partition contains 10,312 graduate-recruitment and 7,148 AI sentences; development contains 2,143 AI sentences; test contains 1,423 AI, 473 Tianchi/cloud, and 1,341 public-institution sentences. The later expansion uses Tianchi/cloud, public-institution, and listed-company source groups. The blinded agreement sample's 26/16/8 counts use these later source groups.

MacroData's operator is Chongqing Maherui Information Technology Co., Ltd. Public-institution records retain source names, titles, URLs, listed publication dates, and geographic fields where available. Publication dates are not collection dates; a complete crawl-window and source-exclusion history has not been recovered. The authors confirm academic-research use permission. Repository notices separately identify the rights in author-created annotations and the restrictions on third-party text.

The Silver training set used by Qwen and the encoder comparison contains 1,910 distinct complete texts among 2,150 records. Repeated records remain weighted in training. A deduplicated training comparison and document-disjoint evaluation would be needed to measure the effects of repeated records and source-document overlap.

\paragraph{Blinded-sample overlap.}
The 50-sentence blinded agreement sample has no ID or normalized complete-text match with the original Silver training/development sets (2,150/169) or the human reference set. Each expanded pool contains 46 of these sentences in training, two in validation, and one in testing; one is absent. Thus the independent annotations can support agreement analysis, but these sentences have already been seen by the expanded models. The \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/tree/main/reproduction/reviewer_supplement_20261004}{membership audit} records the frozen-input hashes and counts.

\subsection{Resource Access and Privacy Screening}
\label{sec:resource-access}

\begin{samepage}
An automated screen examined text fields in 15 public corpus and annotation files, covering 28,635 distinct complete texts. Email, mobile-number, and landline patterns flagged 38, 74, and 50 texts, respectively; categories overlap. No such candidates were found in the human reference set or the additional review sample, but candidates occur in the Silver training/development sets and in the separate blinded agreement sample. These are pattern matches requiring human review, including possible organizational contact details or false positives. The screen covers the specified contact-information patterns; broader personal-information and redistribution-permission checks require separate review. The public audit reports aggregate counts; the masked record-level review list is retained locally for resolution before a privacy-vetted release.\par
\end{samepage}

\paragraph{Model reconstruction.}
JobBERT-zh is the project's released Chinese recruitment-domain encoder, with an inherited CRF used in the reported continuation runs. The exact original tokenizer revision, some pretraining/selection settings, and truncation or windowing counts were not recovered. Its 256-token limit is not a character limit; the reference retains all target spans. The original Qwen Hub commit was not recorded; archived file hashes identify the supplied artifacts. These gaps limit training reconstruction; released predictions can still be rescored.
