\subsection{Encoder Comparison with Chinese Supervision}
\label{sec:external-benchmark-plan}

To assess taxonomy-informed initialization, we compare XLM-R-large~\citep{conneau2020xlmr} and ESCOXLM-R~\citep{zhang2023escoxlmr} with newly initialized nine-label BIO heads on the same Silver training/development split of 2,150 training and 169 development records. Both use AdamW, a learning rate of $2\times10^{-5}$, batch size 8, at most 20 epochs, and seeds 42, 43, and 44. Development typed exact-span F1 determines checkpoint selection.

Both models assign BIO labels to the first subword of each original Chinese character. Predictions from chunks of at most 128 characters, including the final remainder, are combined to cover complete sentences. Tokenizer placeholders preserve whitespace and other positions that would otherwise be discarded; scoring uses original text and offsets. Human-reference sentences are excluded from fitting and checkpoint selection.

We report typed exact-span and typed relaxed-span micro-F1, boundary-only exact-span micro-F1, and macro-F1 over both L/K/S/T and K/S/T. The K/S/T sensitivity average excludes L, which has only two reference spans; it does not change the four-type prediction task. Prediction heads and training budgets differ from those of JobBERT and Qwen, so cross-architecture scores are descriptive rather than controlled comparisons. Paired encoder comparisons use the source-group bootstrap described above (\hyperref[app:c]{Appendix~C}).
