\subsection{Chinese-Supervised Encoder Comparison}
\label{sec:external-benchmark-plan}

To examine whether taxonomy-informed initialization helps this Chinese task, we compare XLM-R-large~\citep{conneau2020xlmr} and ESCOXLM-R~\citep{zhang2023escoxlmr}. Both receive fresh nine-label BIO heads and are fine-tuned on the same B2 split of 2,150 training and 169 development records. They use AdamW, learning rate $2\times10^{-5}$, batch size 8, at most 20 epochs, and seeds 42, 43, and 44. Development typed exact F1 selects each checkpoint.

Both models process the original Chinese characters. The BIO head labels the first subword of each character; predictions from chunks of at most 128 characters are joined, including the remainder of long sentences. Tokenizer placeholders preserve positions for whitespace and otherwise discarded characters. Scoring uses the original text and offsets. The reference is excluded from fitting and checkpoint selection in these runs.

We report typed exact and relaxed F1, boundary-only F1, and both four-type and K/S/T macro-F1. The latter sensitivity summary removes the disproportionate macro weight of the two L spans without changing the four-type prediction task. Training heads and budgets differ from JobBERT and Qwen, so cross-architecture scores remain descriptive. Paired uncertainty is calculated with the same source-group bootstrap used for the main comparisons (Appendix~C).
