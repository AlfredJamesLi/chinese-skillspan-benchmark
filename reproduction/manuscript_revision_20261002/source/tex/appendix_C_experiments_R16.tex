This appendix documents the shared evaluation protocol, uncertainty and error diagnostics, and exploratory expanded-pool results. Scores and differences are calculated before rounding and displayed to three decimals; scientific notation preserves the sign of near-zero interval endpoints. The \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/blob/1d1c24e20ee049fc768034b5baf58fa882e08e2c/reproduction/process_archive_20260924/secondary_review/README.md}{supporting records} provide per-run ledgers, detailed scorer behaviour, and historical protocol comparisons. Those histories use different output formats and demonstration settings and are not treated as controlled estimates of prompt effects.
