\subsection{Dataset Characteristics}
\label{sec:dataset-characteristics}


The annotation layers differ in both type composition and span length (Figure~\ref{fig:length-distributions-r40}). Occupational skills account for 54.6\% of spans in the adopted expanded pool and 67.6\% in the human reference. Language requirements are uncommon in both, accounting for less than 1\% of spans. The median span length is four Unicode code points in the reference and six in the silver training and development sets used by Qwen and the encoder comparison.

The proportion of records with no annotated span also differs: 40.4\% in the silver training set and 48.5\% in development, compared with 26.1\% in the full expanded pool and 5.3\% in the human reference. These differences motivate the analyses by type, span length, and presence of reference spans. \hyperref[app:b]{Appendix B} gives the split-level counts and additional-review distributions.
