\subsection{Fine-Tuning with Silver Labels}
\label{sec:silver-results}

All three Qwen adapters improved exact precision and recall over the no-adapter baseline under the same inference protocol (Table~\ref{tab:gold150-main}, panel A). Mean typed exact micro-F1 increased from 0.361 to $0.540\pm0.035$. Each seed's paired interval against the baseline also excludes zero (Table~\ref{tab:core-paired}), conditional on the recorded runs and reference sentences. Partially rejected responses fall from 69 without an adapter to 2, 4, and 1 after adaptation. Valid spans from partially rejected responses remain in scoring; partial rejection differs from a whole-response failure. The measured benefit is end-to-end extraction, combining content prediction and valid output generation. A format-controlled intervention would be needed to separate those effects.
