\subsection{Fine-Tuning with Silver-plus Labels}
\label{sec:silver-results}

All three Qwen adapters improved exact precision and recall over the no-adapter baseline under the same inference protocol (Table~\ref{tab:gold150-main}, panel A). Mean typed exact micro-F1 increased from 0.361 to $0.540\pm0.035$. Each seed's paired interval against the baseline also excludes zero (Table~\ref{tab:core-paired}), conditional on the recorded runs and reference sentences. This consistency across seeds supports end-to-end task adaptation with the supplied Silver supervision on the selected reference. It does not isolate semantic extraction from output-format learning: partially rejected responses decrease from 69 without an adapter to 2, 4, and 1 in the three adapted runs, under the same parser.
