\subsection{Fine-Tuning with Silver Labels}
\label{sec:silver-results}

All three Qwen adapters improved typed exact-span micro-F1 over the unadapted baseline under the same inference protocol (Table~\ref{tab:core-paired}; Table~\ref{tab:gold150-main}, panel A). The adapted mean was $0.540\pm0.035$, compared with 0.361 without an adapter. The paired seed-mean difference was 0.179, with a 95\% interval of $[0.111, 0.242]$; the three seed-specific intervals were also above zero. Adaptation also increased the mean scores under relaxed and boundary-only matching.

The adapters also produced fewer responses containing rejected span candidates than the unadapted model. These end-to-end scores jointly reflect output validity and span extraction. Detailed parser outcomes accompany the reproduction materials.
