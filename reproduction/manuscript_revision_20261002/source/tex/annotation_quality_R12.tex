Two assisted-review studies assessed generated labels with machine suggestions visible. In a post-generation sample of 100 sentences, separate from the initial 100 reviewed records, agreement between machine-prefilled and human-reviewed annotations reached typed exact-span F1 of 0.959. Two reviewers separately annotated an additional 150-sentence sample, achieving pre-adjudication typed exact-span F1 of 0.816 (\hyperref[app:b]{Appendix B}).

Three annotators independently labeled the same 50-sentence sample drawn from sentences considered for silver annotation, with one sentence per advertisement identifier. They worked in separate Doccano projects with familiarization and practice materials~\citep{klie2024annotationquality}; machine suggestions and other annotators' labels were hidden throughout. Each annotator could consult the study handbook and revise their own labels during individual self-review. Agreement was calculated after self-review, excluding familiarization and practice sentences (\hyperref[sec:blinded-agreement-details]{Appendix B}).

\Needspace{2\baselineskip}
Mean pairwise typed exact-span F1 was 0.818, compared with 0.850 when matching required only identical boundaries (Table~\ref{tab:quality-main}). The difference indicates additional disagreement over type assignment. Pairwise span F1 compares complete annotations symmetrically~\citep{hripcsak2005agreement}, while character-level coefficients assess agreement at individual source positions~\citep{artstein2008agreement}. Across the three annotators, nominal Krippendorff's $\alpha$ was 0.907. The 0.818 agreement was measured on a separate 50-sentence sample and does not provide an independent estimate of annotation reliability for the 150-sentence reference.

The adopted expanded pool contains silver labels for 49 of the 50 blinded-study sentences. A post hoc comparison with each annotator's labels on these sentences yielded mean typed exact-span micro-F1 of 0.709; \hyperref[app:b]{Appendix B} gives per-annotator results and compares the alternative pool.
