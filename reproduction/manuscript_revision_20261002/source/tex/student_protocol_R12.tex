\subsection{Training and Inference}

Supervised checkpoints are selected by typed exact-span F1 on their respective silver development sets. JobBERT-zh + CRF continues training the released encoder and inherited CRF, using supervision informed by handbook revisions, model-generated labels, and human adjudication. \hyperref[sec:jobbert-label-versions]{Appendix C} compares the supervision versions.

Qwen starts from pretrained Instruct weights with a fresh LoRA adapter for each of seeds 42, 43, and 44. Training uses a fixed system instruction, user template, and five schematic examples. Targets specify the literal span text, its type, and an index identifying which occurrence of that text in the sentence is intended (0 denotes the first occurrence). The loss is computed over the assistant's JSON output and closing turn token. Training uses BF16 precision. JobBERT and Qwen use early stopping with a patience of two epochs; Table~\ref{tab:training-settings} lists the other training settings.

XLM-R-large and ESCOXLM-R use AdamW and seeds 42, 43, and 44. BIO labels are assigned to each original Chinese character's first subword. Predictions from chunks of at most 128 source characters, including the remainder, cover complete sentences and are scored against the original text and offsets. \hyperref[app:c]{Appendix C} gives the tokenizer handling needed to preserve source positions.

Each generative request contains one sentence's identifier and text, shared instructions based on handbook v4.2.12, and five fixed examples. A common parser maps the returned literal spans and occurrence indices to Unicode-code-point offsets. Adapted and unadapted Qwen share the base checkpoint, prompts, parser, BF16 precision, deterministic decoding, and a 2,048-token output limit. Other prompted configurations share the instructions and use the inference settings in Table~\ref{tab:model-identity}, including the output budgets used for DeepSeek with and without thinking.
