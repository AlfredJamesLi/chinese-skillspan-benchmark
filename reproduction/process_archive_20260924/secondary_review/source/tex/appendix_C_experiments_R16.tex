\paragraph{Scope of supplementary materials.}
This appendix reports the current shared-guideline configurations, per-seed Qwen results, and frozen-prediction diagnostics. Historical JSON-offset, training-source, demonstration, and cleaning experiments are collected with their complete outcomes in the companion reproduction materials. The archived Qwen JSON-offset study uses a short instruction without demonstrations and reports typed exact micro-F1 of $0.122\pm0.009$. The current shared-guideline study uses five schematic examples and occurrence-based span output, with LoRA reaching $\QwenLoraExact$ against the matched no-adapter baseline of $\QwenBaselineExact$ (Table~\ref{tab:gold150-main}, panel A). The \href{https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/blob/main/REPRODUCIBILITY.md}{reproduction guide} links each result to its corresponding protocol.
