# External recruitment experiments

These protocols and results were previously included in the Chinese-SkillSpan manuscript. They are preserved here as supplementary experiments so the paper can focus on Chinese annotation, independent agreement, and Chinese model evaluation. The historical source files also contain the Chinese encoder section; that section remains in the current paper with updated uncertainty calculations.

No experimental result has been deleted from its source archive or changed by moving this material. The public-subset SkillSpan/German extraction, Kompetencer classification, and NNOSE retrieval protocols retain their original distinctions. Their data splits and scoring units do not support matched replication-gap claims against published scores.

The two Gnehm values displayed as 0.891 ± 0.005 come from different seed results, not copied measurements. The common-protocol three-seed XLM-R mean/SD are 0.8907319922/0.0050921871; the five-seed ESCOXLM-R native-recipe mean/SD are 0.8910946542/0.0045019968. Full values accompany the metrics audit.

Original public evidence: [common and native experiment records](https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/tree/main/results_snapshots), [reproduction guide](https://github.com/AlfredJamesLi/chinese-skillspan-benchmark/blob/main/REPRODUCIBILITY.md).
