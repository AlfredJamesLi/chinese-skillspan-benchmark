# Expanded-Silver access clarification, 17 October 2026

This clarification supersedes earlier wording that promised complete expanded-Silver texts and labels through the corresponding author for confidential peer review.

For the adopted 9,540-record and alternative 9,646-record pools, split identifiers and selected outputs are public. Complete texts and labels are not distributed with the current release. Access to third-party texts is subject to their original providers' access and reuse terms; current provider releases may not reproduce the exact historical expanded pools. Author-granted permissions for annotations and code do not extend to the underlying third-party text.

The available materials do not, by themselves, supply every input needed to retrain the expanded-pool models. Recalculation from released predictions and access to selected model artifacts remain separate from availability of complete training inputs. This clarification makes no new promise of confidential distribution and does not claim that the journal has accepted these access limitations.
